Editor · Map places · from the refinement of “Map pins, cameras and frames” · asked Tue 11:41

Can a map pin hold quest steps, or only point to a quest?

What's going on

The editor is getting pins on maps: a place with a kind, a position and a label. While planning it, the refiner hit a choice the directives don’t settle. Quests need places to happen at, and a pin is the obvious anchor. Either the pin only points to a quest record that lives elsewhere, or the pin also stores the quest steps that happen there. The first keeps quests in one place; the second is quicker to author on the map.

Why the decider didn't decide alone

No directive and no earlier decision covers where quest data lives. It also shapes how you will write quests later, which is your design work, not engineering. Its confidence was 0.62, under the 0.85 it needs.

Options

AIRecommendation

Pins only point to a quest. Your canon rules keep every record in one file and treat views as lenses, never copies; quest steps on pins would be a copy. If authoring on the map feels slow later, a map view that edits the quest record is cheap to add.
